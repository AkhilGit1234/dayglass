Privacy Policy

Dayglass Sign: Consent Forms · Effective 21 October 2026

The short version

We do not collect any data. Dayglass Sign has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising, no tracking and no third-party SDKs. Your forms stay on your device and in your own iCloud.

Information you store in the app

The app lets you record client details, answers to health and consent questions, signatures and photos of ID. This information is stored on your device. If you are signed in to iCloud, it is also stored in your own private iCloud database so it can sync between your devices. That storage is provided by Apple under Apple’s Privacy Policy. The developer cannot access it.

Camera, Face ID and passcode

The camera is used only when you choose to photograph a client’s ID; the photo is kept with that form. Face ID, Touch ID and passcode checks are performed by iOS — the app only learns whether the check succeeded and never receives biometric data.

Purchases

Dayglass Sign Pro is sold through the App Store. Apple processes the payment; we do not receive your payment details.

Sharing and export

Information leaves the app only when you choose to share, print, email or export it.

Your responsibilities as a business

When you use Dayglass Sign to collect your clients’ information, you are responsible for that information under the laws that apply to you (for example GDPR, India’s DPDP Act or US state privacy laws), including telling clients how you use it. Dayglass Sign is not intended for medical practices or HIPAA-covered health records.

Deleting data

Delete individual forms or clients in the app. Deleting the app removes data stored on the device. To remove data from iCloud, go to Settings › [your name] › iCloud › Manage Storage › Dayglass Sign.

Children

The app is a business tool and is not directed at children.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date. Questions: dailyglassapp@gmail.com.
