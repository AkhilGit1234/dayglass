Privacy Policy

Dayglass Bloom · Effective 21 October 2026

Short version: Dayglass Bloom does not collect, store or share any of your personal data. There is no account, no ads, no analytics and no tracking. Everything you add stays on your devices.

What stays on your device

Your plants, photos, notes, watering history and reminder settings are stored only on your iPhone, in the app's own storage. They are never sent to the developer or to any server we run. We don't run any servers.

Photos

When you add a photo, you choose it with Apple's photo picker. Bloom only receives the photo you choose and keeps a resized copy on your device. Bloom does not have access to the rest of your photo library.

Plant identification

If you use “Identify from photo”, Bloom shows Apple's Visual Look Up on the photo you picked. Any lookup is performed by Apple under Apple's Privacy Policy. Bloom does not receive or store the result. You choose the species yourself.

Reminders

Watering reminders are local notifications scheduled on your iPhone. No push service or server is involved.

Widgets and Apple Watch

Widgets read your plant list from the app's private shared storage on your iPhone. The Apple Watch app receives your plant list directly from your iPhone using Apple's WatchConnectivity, and keeps a copy only on your watch.

Purchases

Bloom Pro is a one-time in-app purchase processed entirely by Apple. We do not receive your name, email or payment details.

Children

Bloom doesn't collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Deleting your data

Delete a plant in the app, or delete the app to remove all of its data from your device.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
