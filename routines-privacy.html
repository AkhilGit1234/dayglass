Privacy Policy

Dayglass Routines · Effective 27 October 2026

The short version

We do not collect any data. Dayglass Routines has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising, no tracking and no third-party SDKs. Everything you add stays on your device.

Information you store in the app

You can enter first names or nicknames for family members, routines and their steps, photos you choose for steps, and a record of finished routines for the star chart. This information is stored only on your device. It is not sent to us or to anyone else.

Camera and photos

The camera is used only when you choose to take a photo for a step. When you choose a picture from your library, iOS shows its own picker and the app receives only the photo you pick. Photos are kept with that step on your device.

Face ID, Touch ID and passcode

The grown-up lock uses iOS to check Face ID, Touch ID or your device passcode. The app only learns whether the check succeeded and never receives biometric data.

Children

Dayglass Routines is made for families, and children use Kid Mode. The app collects no personal information from children or anyone else, shows no ads, has no in-app links to the web or social media, and purchases are behind the App Store’s own confirmation (and Ask to Buy, where set up).

Purchases

Dayglass Routines Pro is sold through the App Store. Apple processes the payment; we do not receive your payment details.

Deleting data

Delete a routine from its editor. Deleting the app removes everything it stored on your device.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date. Questions: dailyglassapp@gmail.com.
