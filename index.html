How do I add a Dayglass Soon widget?

Touch and hold an empty area of your Home Screen, tap Edit → Add Widget, search for Dayglass Soon and choose a size. For the Lock Screen, touch and hold the Lock Screen, tap Customize → Lock Screen and add it under the clock.

How do I choose which event a widget shows?

Touch and hold the widget, tap Edit Widget and pick an event. Leave it empty to always show what's coming up next.

How do reminders work?

Open the app, tap the gear icon and turn on Event Reminders. You'll get a reminder at 9:00 AM the day before and on the day of each countdown, and on streak milestones.

My events don't appear on Apple Watch.

Open Dayglass Soon on your iPhone, then on your watch. Events sync automatically when both are nearby with Bluetooth on. Adding or editing any event also triggers a sync.

Where is my data stored?

Only on your devices. There are no accounts and nothing is sent to us. Your events are included in your regular iPhone backups.
