Common questions

How many plants are free?
Three, with every feature included. Bloom Pro is a one-time purchase that unlocks unlimited plants. There's no subscription.

How do I restore Bloom Pro on a new iPhone?
Open Bloom → Settings (gear icon) → Restore Purchase, signed in with the same Apple Account.

I'm not getting reminders.
Check Bloom → Settings → Watering reminders is on, and that notifications for Bloom are allowed in the iPhone Settings app. Reminders arrive on the day a plant is due, at the time you choose.

The soil is still wet. What should I do?
Tap the hourglass (or “Soil still wet” on the reminder) to remind you again in 1–3 days.

How do I add the widget?
Touch and hold the Home Screen → Edit → Add Widget → Bloom. Tap 💧 on the widget to log a watering.

The Apple Watch app is empty.
Open Bloom on your iPhone once while your watch is nearby. It sends your plants to the watch.

Where is my data stored?
Only on your devices. See the Privacy Policy.

Is Plant Doctor a diagnosis?
No. It's general care guidance that suggests what to check first. Every plant and home is different.
