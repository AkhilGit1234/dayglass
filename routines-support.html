Getting started

How do I set up my first routine?

Tap New routine, choose who it’s for (or add a kid or grown-up), then pick one of the 20 ready-made routines or start from scratch. Every routine opens in the editor so you can make it yours before saving.

How do I change a routine?

Tap the pencil on its card. You can rename it, choose who it’s for, the days and a “done by” time, and change each step’s name, picture, timer and hint. Touch and hold a step to drag it into a new place.

Can I use my own photos?

Yes. In the editor, pick a step and tap the camera to take a photo (say, of their real toothbrush), or the photo button to choose one from your library.

What does “Ask twice” do?

For important steps, like medicine or turning the stove off, tapping I did it! shows “Did you really?” first, so steps don’t get skipped by accident.

Kid Mode

How does my child leave a routine?

They can’t by themselves. The lock button at the top left opens the grown-up menu after Face ID, Touch ID or your device passcode. From there you can pause, skip a step, go back a step or end the routine.

What happens if they run over time?

Nothing scary. The timer simply says “Keep going — no rush”, and the pace line stays gentle. A morning routine started after its “done by” time has no deadline at all.

Can I stop my child switching to other apps?

Use Apple’s Guided Access: Settings › Accessibility › Guided Access, then triple-click the side or home button while a routine is open.

Dayglass Routines Pro

What’s free and what’s Pro?

Every feature is free for up to 3 routines, with as many kids and grown-ups as you like. Dayglass Routines Pro is a one-time in-app purchase that unlocks unlimited routines. There’s no subscription.

How do I restore my purchase?

Tap New routine, then the Unlimited with Pro row, then Restore purchase. Use the same Apple Account you bought it with.

Does Pro work for my family?

Yes. Pro supports Family Sharing, so family members in your Apple family group get it on their devices too.

How do I ask for a refund?

Refunds are handled by Apple at reportaproblem.apple.com.

Your data

Do routines sync between my devices?

Not yet. In this version routines and stars are kept on the device where you made them. Syncing through your own iCloud is planned.

Do you collect any data?

No. There are no accounts, no analytics, no ads and no tracking. See the Privacy Policy.

Accessibility

Kid Mode works with VoiceOver (each new step is announced) and Reduce Motion, and uses big, high-contrast text throughout. If something doesn’t work well for your child, please tell us.
