Dayglass Sign: Consent Forms

Client consent and waiver forms for tattoo, piercing, PMU, brow & lash studios, salons, spas, fitness and tutoring. Unlimited waivers. Pay once. Works offline — no login, no outages.

Need help? Email dailyglassapp@gmail.com — we usually reply within 1–2 business days.

How much does it cost?

Every feature is free for your first 5 signed forms. Dayglass Sign Pro is a one-time purchase for unlimited forms. No subscription, no per-form fees. Pro works with Family Sharing.

I reinstalled the app. How do I get Pro back?

Open Settings › Dayglass Sign Pro › Restore Purchase, signed in with the same Apple ID you used to buy it.

Where are my forms stored? Are they backed up?

On your device, and in your own private iCloud if you are signed in to iCloud. They sync between your iPhone and iPad. We never see them.

How do I export everything?

Settings › Your Data › Export All. You get a .zip with every signed PDF plus waivers.csv and clients.csv. Exporting is always free.

How do I stop clients leaving the form?

Kiosk mode (on by default) keeps clients inside the form; leaving needs Face ID or your passcode. For the strongest lock, use Guided Access: Settings › Accessibility › Guided Access, then triple-click the top or Home button while a form is open.

Is this suitable for medical practices?

No. Dayglass Sign is not intended for medical practices or HIPAA-covered health records. The templates are examples, not legal advice — edit them for your state or country.
