Dayglass: Task Planner

Privacy Policy · Effective 15 October 2026

The short version

Dayglass does not collect, send or share any of your data. Your tasks stay on your device.

What the app stores

Your tasks (title, priority, due date, reminder and completion time) are saved only on your iPhone or iPad. We have no servers and no accounts, so we cannot see your tasks.

Notifications

If you turn on a reminder, Dayglass asks permission to send notifications. Reminders are scheduled locally on your device and are not sent through any server.

Export

The export feature creates a file only when you tap it. You choose where that file goes. Nothing is uploaded automatically.

Analytics, ads and third parties

Dayglass contains no analytics, advertising, tracking or third-party code.

Deleting your data

You can clear completed tasks inside the app, or delete the app to remove all of its data from your device.

Children

Dayglass collects no personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new date.
