Privacy Policy

Dayglass Soon: Countdown · Effective October 18, 2026

Dayglass Soon does not collect, store, or share any personal data. Everything you add stays on your own devices.

Information we collect

None. The app has no accounts, no sign-in, no analytics, no advertising, and no tracking. We do not operate servers that receive your information.

Your events

The events you create (titles, dates, emoji, colors, and notes) are stored only on your iPhone, inside the app. They are shared on your device with the app's widgets so they can display your countdowns.

  • Apple Watch: if you use the Apple Watch app, your events are sent directly from your iPhone to your paired Apple Watch using Apple's WatchConnectivity. They do not pass through our servers.
  • Backups: like other app data, your events may be included in your device backups (for example, iCloud Backup), which are managed by Apple under Apple's privacy policy.

Notifications

If you turn on reminders, the app schedules local notifications on your device. They are created and delivered on your device only; no notification data is sent to us.

Third parties

The app contains no third-party SDKs, analytics, or advertising frameworks, and we do not sell or share any data.

Children's privacy

The app is suitable for all ages and does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children.

Deleting your data

You can delete any event inside the app. Deleting the app removes all of its data from your device.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
